# Noise Rules and Event Permits — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Quiet hours
Residential quiet hours are **10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.** on weekdays and **11:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.** on Fridays, Saturdays, and the night before a city holiday.

## Construction noise
Construction work using power tools is allowed only from **7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.** Monday to Saturday. Construction noise is not allowed on Sundays without a variance.

## Amplified sound permits
Outdoor events with amplified sound in a park or street require an **Amplified Sound Permit**. Apply at least **14 days** before the event. The permit fee is **$35**, and amplified sound must end by **9:00 p.m.**

## Complaints and penalties
Report noise complaints to the non-emergency line, 311. A first violation results in a written warning. A second violation within 12 months carries a **$100** fine, and later violations carry a **$250** fine.
