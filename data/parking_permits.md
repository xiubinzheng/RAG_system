# Residential Parking Permits — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Who is eligible
Residents whose primary address is inside a designated Residential Parking Zone (Zones A through F) may apply. Each household may hold up to **two** resident permits at a time.

## How to apply
Residents can apply **online through the Maplewood Resident Portal** or in person at the Transportation Office, 120 Elm Street, Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Applications must include a current vehicle registration showing the in-zone address and one proof of residency dated within the last **60 days** (such as a utility bill or lease).

## Fees and validity
An annual resident permit costs **$45**. A second permit for the same household costs **$75**. Residents aged 65 or older pay a reduced fee of **$20** per permit. Permits are valid from **September 1 through August 31** and are not prorated.

## Visitor passes
Each permit-holding household may buy up to **20 one-day visitor passes per month** at **$2 each**.

## Enforcement
Vehicles parked in a zone without a valid permit between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. on weekdays may receive a **$40** citation.
