# Snow Removal and Winter Parking — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Snow emergencies
The city declares a **Snow Emergency** when **3 inches** or more of snow is forecast. Alerts go out through the **MapleAlert** text service (text SNOW to 55123) and a flashing blue light on top of City Hall.

## Plowing priority
Streets are plowed in this order: **(1)** hospital and fire station routes, **(2)** bus routes and school zones, **(3)** residential streets. Residential streets are plowed within **36 hours** after snowfall ends.

## Parking during a snow emergency
During a Snow Emergency, parking is banned on **red-signed Snow Routes**. Vehicles left there are towed to the lot at **12 Depot Street**. The towing fee is **$125** plus **$15 per day** of storage.

## Sidewalks
Property owners must clear sidewalks within **24 hours** after snowfall ends. The fine for an uncleared sidewalk is **$50** for the first offense. Residents aged 70 or older or with a disability can sign up for the free **Snow Buddy** volunteer program before **November 15**.

## Free sand and salt
Residents may take up to **two 5-gallon buckets** of sand-salt mix per storm from the Public Works yard at 300 Mill Road.

## Unusual rules
- Snowmen taller than **8 feet** require a **$5 Snow Sculpture Permit**. Snowmen built on sidewalks count as obstructions and must be relocated within 24 hours.
- Snowball fights are legally protected in **Cedar Park** from **3:00 to 5:00 p.m.** on snow days. Throwing at City Council members is allowed only if they are participating.
