# Renting City Facilities and Park Pavilions — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## What can be rented
The city rents three types of city-owned property to the public: **park pavilions**, the **Maplewood Community Center ballroom**, and **meeting rooms** at the Main Library. City Hall offices, fire stations, and public works yards are **not** available for rent.

## How to reserve
Reservations are made through the **Maplewood Parks & Facilities Reservation Portal** or in person at the Parks Office, 45 Lake Avenue. Bookings open **6 months** in advance and must be made at least **10 days** before the event. Maplewood residents may book **2 weeks earlier** than non-residents.

## Rental fees
- Park pavilion: **$60** for a 4-hour block for residents, **$90** for non-residents
- Community Center ballroom: **$400** per day, plus a **$250** refundable security deposit
- Library meeting room: **free** for registered nonprofits; **$25 per hour** for other groups

## Cancellations
Cancellations made **30 or more days** before the event receive a full refund minus a **$15** processing fee. Cancellations made later than that forfeit **50%** of the rental fee. Deposits are refunded within **21 days** after the event if no damage is found.

## Rules
Alcohol is allowed only in the Community Center ballroom and requires a **Special Event Alcohol Permit** and proof of liability insurance of at least **$1 million**. Events must end by **10:00 p.m.** Renters are charged the actual cost of repairing any damage to city property.
