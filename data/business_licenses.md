# General Business Licenses — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Who needs a license
Any business operating within city limits needs a **General Business License**, including home-based businesses. Exceptions: children under **16** running occasional businesses (such as lemonade stands or lawn mowing) and garage sales held **no more than 3 times** per year.

## How to apply
Apply through the **Maplewood Business Hub** online, or at the City Clerk's office, 1 Civic Plaza. New applications are typically approved within **7 business days**.

## Fees
The license fee is based on the number of employees:
- 0–5 employees: **$75** per year
- 6–25 employees: **$200** per year
- More than 25 employees: **$500** per year

Licenses expire on **December 31** each year. Renewals made after **January 31** have a **$35** late fee.

## Home-based businesses
Home businesses may have no more than **2 non-resident employees** on site and no more than **8 customer visits** per day. Exterior signs are limited to **2 square feet**.

## Food trucks
Food trucks need a separate **Mobile Vending Permit** (**$300** per year) and may not park within **200 feet** of a restaurant during its business hours.
