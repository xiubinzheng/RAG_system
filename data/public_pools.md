# Public Pools and Splash Pads — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Season and hours
The city operates **two outdoor pools** (Riverside Pool and Oak Hill Pool) from **Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day**. Pools are open **noon to 7 p.m.** daily. The **Cedar Park splash pad** is free and open **10 a.m. to 8 p.m.**

## Admission
- Residents: **$4** per day for adults, **$2** for children aged 3–17, free for children under 3
- Non-residents: **$8** for adults, **$4** for children
- Season pass: **$90** per family of up to 5 residents

## Rules
Children under **10** must be accompanied in the water by someone aged **16 or older**. To use the deep end or the diving board, swimmers must pass a **swim test**: swim **25 yards** without stopping and tread water for **1 minute**.

## Weather closures
Pools close for **30 minutes** after any thunder or lightning. If a pool closes for weather within **one hour** of a guest's arrival, the guest receives a free return pass.

## Swim lessons
Swim lessons cost **$45** for a two-week session of 8 classes. Registration opens on **April 20** and residents get **one week** of priority registration.
