# Water and Sewer Billing — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Billing cycle
Water and sewer bills are issued **every two months** (bimonthly). Payment is due **21 days** after the bill date.

## Payment options
Bills can be paid online with no fee by bank transfer, by credit card (a **2.5%** convenience fee applies), by mail, or in person at the Utility Billing Office.

## Late payments
A **$10** late fee is added to balances not paid by the due date. Service may be disconnected when an account is more than **60 days** past due, after a **10-day** written disconnection notice. Reconnection costs **$50**.

## Leak adjustments
Customers may request a one-time leak adjustment once every **24 months**. The repair must be completed within **30 days** of discovering the leak, and a plumber's receipt or photo evidence is required. The adjustment credits **50%** of the water charges above the customer's normal average.

## Payment assistance
Households with income at or below **200%** of the federal poverty level may enroll in the Water Assist program for a **30%** discount on the base service charge.

## Unusual rules
- Customers who can whistle the **Maplewood city anthem** at the Utility Billing Office receive a **5% discount** on their next bill. Limit one performance per account per year.
- Water from the North Reservoir is officially classified as **"slightly enthusiastic"** due to its fizz. It is safe to drink.
