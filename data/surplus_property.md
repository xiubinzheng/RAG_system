# Sale of Surplus City Property — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## What is surplus property
Surplus property is city-owned equipment, vehicles, furniture, or land that a department no longer needs. A department head must declare an item surplus, and items valued over **$25,000** also require **City Council approval** before sale.

## How items are sold
Vehicles, equipment, and furniture are sold through **online public auctions** held **quarterly** (in January, April, July, and October). Items are sold **"as is, where is"** with no warranty. Surplus **land** is sold by **sealed bid**, with a minimum bid set at **90%** of the appraised value.

## Who may buy
The public may bid. **City employees and their immediate family members may not bid** on surplus property. Before a public auction, items are first offered to other government agencies and local nonprofits for **15 days**.

## Payment and pickup
Winning bidders must pay within **3 business days** by cashier's check, wire transfer, or credit card (a **3%** fee applies to card payments). Items must be picked up from the City Warehouse at **780 Industrial Way** within **10 business days**, or they are forfeited and may be resold.

## Disposal
Items that receive no bids after **two** auctions may be recycled or donated. Computers and phones are wiped of all data by the IT Department before sale.

## Unusual rules
- The decommissioned 1972 fire truck known as **"Big Red"** is not surplus property. It was declared an **honorary citizen** in 1998 and cannot be sold.
- Surplus **rubber chickens** from the Parks Department clown program are sold only as a set of **12** and only at the **April** auction.
