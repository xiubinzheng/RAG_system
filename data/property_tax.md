# Property Tax Payments and Exemptions — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Tax rate and bills
The Maplewood property tax rate is **$11.40 per $1,000** of assessed value. Tax bills are mailed on **July 15** each year and may be paid in full or in **two installments**, due **October 1** and **March 1**.

## Assessments
Properties are reassessed every **4 years**. Owners who disagree with an assessment may file an appeal with the Board of Assessment Review within **45 days** of the assessment notice. Appeals are free and hearings are held on **Tuesday evenings**.

## Exemptions
- **Homestead exemption:** owner-occupied homes receive a **$30,000** reduction in assessed value. Apply once; it renews automatically.
- **Senior freeze:** homeowners aged **67 or older** with household income under **$58,000** may freeze their assessed value.
- **Veterans:** veterans with a service-connected disability rating of **50% or more** pay no city property tax on their primary home.

## Late payments
Unpaid balances accrue interest at **1% per month**. After **three years** of unpaid taxes, the property may be offered at a tax lien sale.

## Unusual rules
- Houses shaped like a **giant fruit or vegetable** qualify for a **10% Novelty Architecture Credit**. The only current recipient is the pineapple house on Fern Lane.
- Taxes may not be paid in pumpkins. Pumpkins are accepted only for parking permits.
