# Curbside Recycling and Yard Waste — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Collection schedule
Recycling is collected **every other week** on the same weekday as trash collection. Yard waste is collected weekly from **April 1 through November 30**. Carts must be at the curb by **6:00 a.m.** on collection day and removed by 8:00 p.m. the same day.

## What goes in the blue recycling cart
- Clean paper, flattened cardboard, and newspaper
- Plastic bottles and containers labeled **#1, #2, and #5**
- Aluminum and steel cans
- Glass bottles and jars are **not** accepted curbside; take them to the Glass Drop-Off Center at 900 Quarry Road.

## What is not accepted
Plastic bags, styrofoam, food-soiled paper, batteries, and electronics must not go in the recycling cart. Plastic bags can be returned to participating grocery stores.

## Replacement carts
A damaged or stolen cart is replaced **free of charge** once per calendar year. Additional replacements cost **$65**. Request a cart by calling 311.

## Contamination
A cart found with non-recyclable items receives a warning tag. After **three** tags in 12 months, the household is charged a **$25** contamination fee.

## Unusual rules
- Recycling carts must be placed with the lid hinge facing **north**. Carts facing other directions are collected, but the driver is allowed to sigh audibly.
- Items reported as **haunted** (confirmed by **two witnesses**) are collected separately on any **Friday the 13th**.
