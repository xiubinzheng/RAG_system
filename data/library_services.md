# Maplewood Public Library Services — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Library cards
Library cards are **free** for anyone who lives, works, or attends school in Maplewood. Non-residents may buy a card for **$40 per year**. Children of any age may get their own card with a parent's signature.

## Borrowing limits
Cardholders may borrow up to **30 items** at once. Books are loaned for **3 weeks**, DVDs for **1 week**, and **museum passes** for **3 days**. Items may be renewed up to **twice** if no one else has placed a hold.

## Fines
Maplewood Library is **fine-free** for books and DVDs. However, an item more than **45 days** overdue is billed at its replacement cost, and the card is blocked until it is returned. Late museum passes cost **$10 per day**.

## Unusual items you can borrow
The "Library of Things" lends **telescopes**, **sewing machines**, **ukuleles**, **pickleball sets**, and a **thermal camera** for finding home energy leaks. These items are limited to **one per card** at a time.

## Hours
The Main Library is open **9 a.m. to 8 p.m.** Monday to Thursday, and **10 a.m. to 5 p.m.** Friday to Sunday. It is closed on city holidays.
