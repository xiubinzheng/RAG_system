# Residential Building Permits — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## When a permit is required
A building permit is required for new structures, additions, structural changes, decks higher than **30 inches** above grade, fences taller than **6 feet**, and any new electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work. Painting, flooring, and replacing cabinets do not require a permit.

## How to apply
Submit an application, a site plan, and construction drawings to the Department of Building Safety. Applications are accepted **online only** through the Maplewood ePermits system.

## Review times
Standard residential plan review takes up to **15 business days**. Minor projects such as water heater replacements qualify for **same-day over-the-counter** approval.

## Fees
The base permit fee is **$150** plus **$8 per $1,000** of estimated project value. Plan review fees are non-refundable.

## Inspections
Inspections must be scheduled at least **24 hours** in advance. A permit expires if work does not begin within **180 days** of issuance.
