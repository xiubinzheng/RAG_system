# Public Records Requests — City of Maplewood

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** This document was written for a software demo. It is not official guidance from any real government. "City of Maplewood" is a made-up city.

## Who may request records
**Any person** may request city records. Requesters do not need to state a reason or be a Maplewood resident.

## How to submit a request
Submit requests by email to records@maplewood.example, through the online Records Request Form, or in writing to the City Clerk at City Hall, 1 Civic Plaza.

## Response time
The City Clerk will acknowledge a request within **5 business days** and will either provide records or give an estimated completion date. Complex requests may be extended by up to **10 additional business days** with written notice.

## Fees
Electronic copies are **free** for the first **100 pages**. Paper copies cost **$0.15 per page**. Staff time beyond **two hours** is billed at **$30 per hour**.

## Exemptions
Records may be withheld or redacted if they contain personal medical information, active law-enforcement investigations, or Social Security numbers. Denials will cite the specific exemption and explain how to appeal to the City Attorney within **30 days**.
