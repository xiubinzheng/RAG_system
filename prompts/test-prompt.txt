As an owner-occupied homeowner, you qualify for the Homestead exemption, which provides a
$30,000 reduction in assessed value [property_tax.md]. Additionally, because you are at
least 67 years old, you may qualify for the Senior freeze to freeze your assessed value if
your household income is under $58,000 [property_tax.md]. If you pay late, unpaid balances
accrue interest at 1% per month, and after three years of unpaid taxes, the property may
be offered at a tax lien sale [property_tax.md].
